Problem Statement

Many people—especially when walking alone—feel unsafe or risk being unable to quickly call for help in emergencies.

Goal: Build an application that increases personal safety through smart alerts, route tracking, and wearable device integration.

Quick question...

“So… do you have a wearable device?”

Iteration 1 – Safety Check Prototype

  • User opens app and clicks “Start Journey”.
  • App prompts user at set intervals to confirm safety.
  • If no confirmation for 2 intervals → local alarm sounds.
  • When home, user clicks “I’m home” and confirms with password.

Iteration 2 – Smart Route & Notifications

  • User selects journey on map.
  • App tracks deviations or long stops.
  • User adds emergency contacts.
  • Contacts receive SMS with live location link and event updates.

App becomes location-aware and connected to others.

Iteration 3 – Wearable & Sensor Integration

  • Connects with wearables (Garmin, Apple Watch, Wear OS... IF YOU DO HAVE IT).
  • Detects falls, sudden motion, or stress signals (e.g., high heart rate).
  • Automatically triggers SOS alerts on detection.

Safety checks move beyond manual input — the app “feels” danger.

Iteration 4 – Advanced Safety Features

  • Choose which events trigger SOS messages.
  • Automatic call to 112 when SOS triggered.
  • Start voice recording upon fall or struggle detection.

Fully-featured safety system.

Iteration 5 – Polish & Testing

  • Test with real journeys and wearable data.
  • Adjust sensitivity to reduce false alarms.
  • Polish UI and performance.
  • Prepare final demo, video and poster.
